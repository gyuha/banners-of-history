Historical Background
Date & Era

1140 CE · Southern Song · Shaoxing era, Song–Jin wars
Yue Fei’s northern offensive met a renewed Jin effort to concentrate against his forward positions.

The Opposing Armies

Yue Fei commanded Song forces against a Jin army led by Wanyan Zongbi and other officers. Both sides employed infantry, cavalry and varied contingents during their prolonged struggle for the central plains.

Why They Fought

After peace efforts broke down, Jin again advanced south. Yue Fei moved into Henan and distributed troops among several towns. Jin sought to concentrate against his forward positions and main force, making Yancheng a key battlefield.

The Battle

The History of Song describes resistance to Jin cavalry and fighting by Yue Yun and others, with cooperation between infantry and horse. Later descriptions disagree on the form, scale and tactics of Tiefutu and Guaizima formations; the fighting cannot be reduced to one universal anti-cavalry trick.

Historical Outcome

Song won engagements around Yancheng, but court decisions and the wider situation halted the offensive and Yue Fei was ordered back. These victories neither ended the Song–Jin confrontation nor destroyed the Jin state.
