Raise your banner. Rewrite the battle.

Song of Ashes — Historical Battles Demo is a real-time action and army-command game. Create your commander, choose a portrait, name your company, and carry your own crest onto banners and shields.

20 battles across history
10 Tactical History battles: lead an outnumbered force and use pikes, shields, missiles, cavalry, terrain, and lieutenant commands to win.
10 Heroic Musou battles: fight as a powerful commander with a small elite guard, from Xiang Yu and Zhao Yun to Huo Qubing, Wei Qing, Chen Qingzhi, Li Shimin, and Yue Fei.

Each battle includes its era, opposing armies, causes, course, historical outcome, tactical advice, and objectives. Heroic abilities and playable army sizes are adaptations for the game.

Your commander, your company

Choose from 40 portraits and mix eight equipment sets. Weapon types change moves and skills; appearance equipment provides no stat bonuses. Design a heraldic crest, including historical faction characters.

Learn, command, achieve

The field manual covers troops, weapon skills, terrain, and battles. Earn five distinct achievements per battle — 100 total, shown with bronze, silver, and gold medals. Use the tactical map to select formations, command your own front, or take over your lieutenants’ units.

Play free in your browser

Desktop and landscape phones. Touch movement, attacks, and command controls are included. Settings can switch Desktop / Phone Landscape and English / 繁體中文. The first launch follows a supported browser language and otherwise uses English. Progress is saved in this browser.

灰燼之歌｜二十場歷史戰役試玩版

建立團長、挑選四十款肖像、命名軍團，讓專屬軍旗與盾徽隨軍出征。八套裝具可自由混搭，武器種類決定招式；外觀裝具沒有能力加成。

十場史實戰術以少勝多，利用山脊、河橋、隘口和兵種協同作戰；你負責主路，副將照顧側路，也能接管其部隊。十場英雄無雙率少量頂級親軍親自破陣，題材涵蓋項羽、霍去病、衛青、陳慶之、李世民與岳飛等人物。

戰場圖鑑收錄登場兵種、武器戰技、地形和戰役；每關五項成就，共一百項，採銅銀金獎章與解鎖提示。每關附年代、朝代、交戰雙方、開戰原因、戰役經過、歷史結局、打法及勝利目標。

電腦和手機皆可在瀏覽器免費遊玩，手機僅支援橫式。設定可切換手機介面及中英文；首次依支援的系統語言選擇，其餘預設英文。進度儲存在目前瀏覽器。歡迎自由捐款支持。
